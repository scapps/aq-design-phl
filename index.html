    Reference level

    The map shows one hour of steady-state concentration. Air-quality standards are statistics over months to years (for example the 99th percentile of daily 1-hour maxima, averaged over 3 years), so one modelled hour is too short to show an exceedance or compliance. The sandbox therefore shows a standard only as a reference level: a mark on the colour scale and a value in the summary.
